# MeetChengdu — SEO Content Plan (Journal)

_Prepared 2026-10-15. Draft for owner review. Nothing here has been published._

## Summary

1. meetchengdu.com is a new domain with no backlinks and no impressions, so head terms like "Chengdu tour guide" are out of reach. Agencies (China Discovery, China Highlights, HelloChinaTrip), marketplaces (Tripadvisor, Viator, GetYourGuide, Klook, HeroGuide) and a wave of 2026 "China for foreigners" blogs hold those positions.
2. What we can win is long-tail, question-shaped queries where competitors are either selling a tour or repeating each other's logistics. The pieces nobody covers well are how to check that a guide is who they say they are, and how to read Chinese-platform evidence.
3. Our moat is the 31-profile evidence directory. Each article should answer a practical question with cited facts, then point to "people who publicly post about this" (specific `/guides/<slug>` pages), never to "the best guide".
4. Top two to draft now: (1) how to hire and verify an English-speaking guide in Chengdu, using the official 2025 guide ID card with its QR code; (2) Panda Base 2026 logistics (passport booking, entry sessions, gates) with a section on when an English docent actually helps.
5. Publish one article every two weeks. Each needs about an hour of owner review, mostly re-checking the facts marked [VERIFY] against official channels, because ticket rules change often.

## Publishing cadence

- **One article every two weeks**, in the order above (P1s first). Topic 3 lands about four weeks out, after both drafts here are live.
- **About one hour of owner review per article**: about 30 minutes re-checking every [VERIFY] item against the official channel or a fresh source, about 15 minutes checking that each guide mention matches the current profile in seed data (status still `unclaimed`/`claimed`, not `removed`), and about 15 minutes on title, SEO description, and internal links.
- **Refresh quarterly**: articles 2, 3, 5 and 7 contain rules and prices that change. Add a visible "Last checked" line and re-check every 90 days, in step with the source-accessibility check in the editorial standards.
- **Measure**: watch Search Console impressions per URL after 4–6 weeks. The anonymous analytics already counts article to guide-card to source click-outs, which is the conversion that matters.
- **Low-effort distribution**: after publishing, link new articles from the relevant guide profiles' related reading (if supported) and from the existing 48-hours article.
