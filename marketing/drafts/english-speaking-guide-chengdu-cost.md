## The short answer

For a private English-speaking guide in Chengdu, expect to pay roughly **¥600–800 (about US$90–120) per group for a standard 8-hour day**, for the guide's time only. Guides booked through agencies and booking platforms are often listed at **US$160–380 a day**, and packages with a private car, tickets or meals cost more.

The headline number matters less than **what it includes**. Two quotes that look far apart are often for different things: one is the guide only, the other includes a car, a driver, fuel and tolls. This page explains how prices are built, so you can compare quotes like for like.

All figures below come from guides' and agencies' own published 2026 prices (see Sources). They're sellers' figures, not a fixed tariff, so always get your own quote in writing.

## Typical prices in 2026

| What you're paying for | Published price range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Guide only, full day (up to 8 hours) | ¥600–800 per group | Chengdu sits in the lower price band, alongside Chongqing and Zhangjiajie |
| Guide only, Beijing or Shanghai (for comparison) | ¥800–1,000 per group | Useful if you're pricing a multi-city trip |
| Guide, half day (4 hours or less) | from about US$90 | Often more than half the day rate |
| Guide booked through an agency or platform, full day | US$160–380 | Excludes tickets; check whether pick-up or a car is included |
| Private car with driver, 8 hours / 100 km | from US$147 (5 seats), US$168 (7 seats) | Fuel, tolls, parking and the driver's meals included |
| Airport transfer, one way | from US$63 (5 seats) | Tianfu (TFU) is much further out than Shuangliu (CTU) |
| Overtime | ¥100–200 per hour | Agree the hourly rate before the day starts |

**The guide's rate is usually per group, not per person.** One published Chengdu price list charges ¥600 for 1–2 people and ¥800 for groups of 10 or more. A family of four often pays little more than a couple.

**Other languages cost more.** The same price list charges ¥800–1,100 a day for French, German, Spanish or Italian, against ¥600–800 for English.

## What a "day" means

Most quotes define a full day as **up to 8 hours** and a half day as **4 hours or less**. Check:

- **When the clock starts.** At your hotel, or at the first sight? A day trip to Leshan can use two hours just on the road.
- **What happens if you run late.** Overtime is charged by the hour (often ¥100 per hour, and up to ¥200).
- **Evening plans.** A hot pot dinner or a Sichuan opera show after a full day usually counts as overtime or a separate booking.

## What is usually *not* included

When a quote is for the guide only, these are normally extra:

- **Transport:** a private car, taxis, DiDi rides, metro fares or train tickets. For a day trip by train, you'll usually pay the guide's fare too.
- **Entrance tickets:** for example about ¥55 for the Chengdu Panda Base and ¥72 for the Sanxingdui Museum. Ask whether you're expected to pay for the guide's ticket.
- **Sightseeing buses and cable cars** inside large sites, such as the ¥30 shuttle at the Panda Base.
- **Meals:** it's normal to buy the guide's lunch, or give a meal allowance, on a full day.
- **Tips** (see below).

Agency packages often include some of these. Neither model is better; just make sure you're comparing the same things.

## Rough budgets for popular days

These show **how the costs add up** using the published figures above. They aren't quotes; prices change, and your guide's will differ.

**Half day at the Panda Base (2 people, by metro or taxi)**
- Guide, half day: from about US$90
- Panda Base tickets: about ¥55 each (plus the guide's, if you're paying)
- Optional sightseeing bus: ¥30 each
- Taxi or DiDi there and back

Read our [Chengdu Panda Base guide](/blog/chengdu-panda-base-guide) for booking with your passport and the best time to go.

**Full day: Panda Base + Sanxingdui with a private car**
- Guide, full day: about ¥600–800
- Car and driver: from US$147 for 8 hours / 100 km. The round trip is often 110–140 km, so allow for a small distance surcharge.
- Tickets: about ¥55 (Panda Base) + ¥72 (Sanxingdui) each

See [Visiting Sanxingdui from Chengdu](/blog/sanxingdui-from-chengdu) for what to see there.

**Day trip to Leshan Giant Buddha**
- *By train:* high-speed trains from Chengdu East reach Leshan in as little as about 40 minutes, from about ¥30 each way in second class. You pay the guide's fare, plus a taxi or bus at the Leshan end.
- *By car:* the round trip is commonly 300 km or more, well beyond the usual 100 km in a day rate, so expect a distance surcharge and probably overtime.

The train is usually the cheaper option for a small group; a car makes more sense for families, older travellers or anyone combining Leshan with other stops.

## Tipping your guide and driver

Tipping isn't part of everyday life in China, and guides aren't supposed to ask for tips. On private tours with foreign visitors, though, a tip at the end is common and appreciated. Published guidance for 2026 suggests about:

- **Guide: ¥100–200 per day** (per group)
- **Driver: ¥50–100 per day** (per group)

Give cash at the end of the day, or the end of the trip, **separately to the guide and the driver**, since they're often paid separately. A red envelope (红包) is a nice touch. If you booked through an agency, ask what's customary.

## Why prices vary so much

- **Licensed guide or not.** Licensed guides carry China's official guide ID card. Our [hiring guide](/blog/how-to-hire-english-speaking-guide-chengdu) explains how to check it.
- **Experience and specialism.** A guide with deep knowledge of Sanxingdui archaeology, Sichuan food or western Sichuan routes can charge more.
- **Season.** Expect higher prices and less availability during Chinese holidays, especially the October Golden Week and Spring Festival.
- **Agency margin.** An agency handles planning, cars, cover if a guide is ill, and payment protection. That's worth something, but it's part of the price.
- **Group size and language,** as above.

## Before you agree a price

- [ ] Guide fee in writing: per group or per person, and in which currency.
- [ ] Start and end times, and the overtime rate.
- [ ] Exactly what's included: car, fuel, tolls, parking, tickets, sightseeing buses, meals.
- [ ] Whether you pay the guide's tickets, train fare and lunch.
- [ ] Deposit, payment method, and the cancellation policy.
- [ ] No unplanned shopping stops (be wary of a price far below the ranges above).
