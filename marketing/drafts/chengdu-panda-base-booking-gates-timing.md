## The short version

The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding is the visit most people plan a Chengdu trip around, and the one most easily spoiled by small mistakes. In 2026 the visit runs on three rules: **book in advance under your passport, arrive at the start of your session, and bring the original passport.** Everything else on this page is about using those rules well.

Rules here change from season to season, and English-language sources don't always agree. We say where they disagree. Check the base's official WeChat account or your booking platform the day before you go.

## Booking with a foreign passport

- **Price.** Several 2026 guides list adult admission at **CNY 55** and an eligible student ticket at **CNY 27**. [VERIFY on the official channel; discount eligibility for foreign students and seniors varies by source.]
- **Where to book.** Through the base's official WeChat mini program or official account (成都大熊猫繁育研究基地), or authorised platforms. Trip.com is the one most often recommended for visitors without a Chinese phone number.
- **How far ahead.** Reservations open up to **14 days** in advance. For ordinary weekdays, one to three days ahead is usually enough. For weekends, Chinese public holidays and the October Golden Week, book one to two weeks ahead.
- **Passport, not ID card.** When the form asks for a document type, choose passport (护照). Your booking is tied to the passport number. Several sources describe the passport itself as your ticket at the gate.
- **On-site tickets.** Sources disagree. Some say there are no on-site sales. Others say a window sells tickets only when unsold inventory remains. Don't build your day around buying at the gate. [VERIFY]

At entry, foreign visitors generally go through staffed verification lanes rather than the ID-card turnstiles. Bring the **original passport** you booked with. A photo or scan may not be accepted.

## Morning and afternoon sessions

Tickets are sold by session, and arriving early won't get you in before your session starts. According to the booking guides we checked:

| Season | Morning entry | Afternoon entry | Park closes |
|---|---|---|---|
| March–October | 7:30–12:00 | 12:00–17:00 | 18:00 |
| November–February | 8:00–12:00 | 12:00–16:30 | 17:30 |

[VERIFY winter times before publishing in November.]

**Choose the morning session.** Every source we reviewed agrees that pandas are most active early, roughly 7:30 to 10:00, around feeding time, and that many are asleep in trees by the afternoon. In summer the heat makes the early start even more important. Arriving shortly before your session opens, rather than "sometime in the morning", is the single biggest improvement you can make.

## South gate or west gate?

The base has two main visitor gates, and English-language guides give fairly consistent advice:

- **South Gate** is the usual choice for first-time visitors and is closer to the nursery areas.
- **West Gate** is often described as a quieter start, though not always the simplest route for a first visit.

Public posts from the base also document the queue. [Fiona](/guides/fiona), a bilingual guide whose public profile focuses almost entirely on the panda base, has posted morning queue reports from the west gate at opening time. Recent posts like hers are a useful real-world check on how busy an entrance is that week.

## Getting around inside

The base is large and hilly. The official **sightseeing bus** runs loops between the South Gate and West Gate, stopping at panda villas, the Sun and Star nursery houses, the Panda Creek Valley and other points. The base lists:

- **CNY 30 per person** for visitors over 1.3 m. Children aged 6 and under, or under 1.3 m, ride free.
- Hours of **7:30–17:30** from March to October and **8:00–17:00** from November to February.
- Tickets through the official mini program or account (up to 14 days ahead) or on site on the day.

A common pattern is to walk the first enclosures while the pandas are active, then use the bus to cross the park.

## Getting there

Most visitors take a taxi or ride-hailing car for an early session, because the first metro trains and a transfer can make a 7:30 start tight. Metro Line 3 serves the area, and a panda shuttle bus connects from the metro. Sources name the nearest station differently, so check the current route in your map app. [VERIFY station name and shuttle route before publishing.] Keep the address in Chinese on your phone: 成都市成华区熊猫大道1375号 (1375 Panda Avenue, Chenghua District).

## When an English-speaking docent helps

You can see pandas perfectly well without a guide. The signs are bilingual and the main routes are easy to follow. A docent earns their fee when you want **context**: which panda is which, how the breeding programme works, why a cub is in one house and not another, and which enclosure is likely to be active right now. They can also save time if you're short on it, travelling with children, or nervous about the booking steps.

Several people in our directory publicly describe panda-base work:

- [Xiao O](/guides/xiao-o) describes herself as a licensed English guide offering bilingual commentary at the base, with near-daily posts about groups from Europe, North America, Australia and Singapore.
- [Betty](/guides/betty) lists certification as a bilingual docent at the base alongside an English tour-guide licence.
- [Fiona](/guides/fiona) lists a guide licence and private-group commentary, and posts individual panda updates and queue reports.
- [Libby](/guides/libby) lists an English tour-guide licence and posts about panda and red-panda outings with visitors.
- [Huage Harry](/guides/huage-harry) publishes English-language videos that include panda visits as part of wider Chengdu culture.

These descriptions come from their public profiles. They aren't endorsements, and they don't tell you about current availability or price. Some profiles mention "fast entry" or VIP access. Ask exactly what that means, whether it is an official channel, and what it costs, before you agree to it.

## Before you book

- [ ] Morning session booked under **passport**, with the passport number checked against the document you'll carry.
- [ ] Original passport packed. A photocopy alone may not be accepted.
- [ ] Session time and gate (south or west) noted, with a taxi or ride-hailing pickup planned to arrive before opening.
- [ ] If you're hiring a docent: identity, credential and price confirmed directly with them. Ask who books the tickets, whether the sightseeing bus is included, and what "fast entry" actually means.
- [ ] Rules re-checked on the official WeChat account or booking platform the day before.
